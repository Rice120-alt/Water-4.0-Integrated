# Security policy

Water 4.0 is a single-player mod. Do not use it in multiplayer or shared
sessions where its behavior could affect other players.

## Reporting a concern

For a suspected security issue, package-integrity problem, unexpected file
write, or malicious modification, contact the maintainer privately through the
same distribution channel used for this repository. Do not publish sensitive
details until the issue has been investigated.

Include:

- the exact Water 4.0 version;
- the SHA-256 of the downloaded archive;
- the affected file and a concise reproduction; and
- relevant log lines with private paths and account information removed.

Do not upload save files, crash dumps, personal paths, or third-party runtime
installers to an issue tracker. The release candidate is not an official Fun
Dog product and is not supported by Fun Dog.
